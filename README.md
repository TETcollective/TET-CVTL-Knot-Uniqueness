## Abstract

We present a game-theoretic model for primordial knot selection in the TET–CVTL vacuum. Knots compete for linking number and helicity under minimal Chern-Simons action and eternal Ising braiding. The unique equilibrium is the three-leaf clover knot with L_k = 6. Numerical simulations with 100 knots confirm robust convergence.
